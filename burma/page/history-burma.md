# Other Online Collections Related to Burma

(Items in bold are the most recommended)

- [**Hathi Trust**](https://www.hathitrust.org/) **is probably the best for finding unique material** that will not appear in other collections.
- For ideas of additional sources, see this [Selected Reference List](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=uc1.b4910607&view=1up&seq=1) by Cecil Hobbs, 17 pages worth of British White Papers considered important to understanding Myanmar.
- [Southeast Asia Visions](http://seasiavisions.library.cornell.edu/) - This digital collection at Cornel University includes hundreds of digitized books related to Southeast Asia. Many, but by no means all, of the books related to Burma are listed below and it is well worth exploring their collection.
- [Burmese Album](https://digitalcollections.colgate.edu/node/540) - This collection of photographs is hosted by Colgate University. "An album of sepia albumen photographs taken in Burma by the expatriate German photographer Philip Adolphe Klier." Read more about this collection [here](https://archives.colgate.edu/repositories/2/resources/359).
- [Burma and British India album, circa 1900s](https://digital.wolfsonian.org/node/59499/object-media) - A collection of photographs from an album made available by The Wolfsonian - Florida International University [photos are untitled on the download list]. The Wolfsonian has [a number of maps, photos, and objects from Burma digitalised](https://digital.wolfsonian.org/solr-search/content?search_api_fulltext=burma&sort_by=search_api_relevance&sort_order=DESC&items_per_page=10).
- [Burma Library](https://www.burmalibrary.org/) - "Classified and annotated links to more than 60,000 full text documents on Burma/Myanmar". See for example the [history](https://www.burmalibrary.org/en/category/history) subsection.
- [The Anglo-Burmese Library](https://www.angloburmeselibrary.com/) - This organization, which requires membership for access to its member-only collection of documents, preserves records of families in colonial-era Burma and maintaining a list of 1942 evacuation and casualties. The library has a [non-member area](https://www.angloburmeselibrary.com/non-members-area.html) with a limited number of materials.
- [Digital Bodleian](https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/) - The Bodleian library at Oxford, has [several digitized materials](https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/search/?q=burma) related to Burma.
- [Pathé Films](https://www.britishpathe.com/) - This archive of film clips has [several dozen clips](https://www.britishpathe.com/search/?searchQuery=burma&page=1) from Burma, including curated lists (mostly from the Second World War) such as: [Burma](https://www.britishpathe.com/groupitem/3258/) | [War in Burma](https://www.britishpathe.com/groupitem/2212/) | [1943, Burma](https://www.britishpathe.com/groupitem/1224/) | [Back from Burma](https://www.britishpathe.com/groupitem/927/)
- [Digital South Asia Library](http://dsal.uchicago.edu/) - This collection, based at the University of Chicago is especially useful for the period when Burma was treated by the British as part of its administration of India, up to 1937.
- **Medical History of British India - This National Library of Scotland collection includes** the [Annual reports on the working of the Harcourt Butler Institute of Public Health, Rangoon 1927-1940](https://digital.nls.uk/indiapapers/browse/archive/74949806?mode=gallery_list&sn=1). It also has reports related to [Vaccination in Burma 1889-1933](https://digital.nls.uk/indiapapers/browse/archive/91546117). See more on the [catalogue entry](https://search.nls.uk/primo-explore/fulldisplay?vid=44NLS_VU1&docid=44NLS_ALMA21452856000004341) for the institute reports, and the [entry](https://search.nls.uk/primo-explore/fulldisplay?vid=44NLS_VU1&docid=44NLS_ALMA21488841460004341) for the vaccination reports. It may take a deeper dive to uncover the mentions of Burma in various reports surveying the whole of British India, such as the [reports of various diseases](https://digital.nls.uk/indiapapers/browse/archive/74466617?from_row=1) which would uncover some interesting tidbits. There is also an excellent selection of [secondary sources on South Asian colonial medical history](https://digital.nls.uk/indiapapers/resources.html) on this webpage. For a quick view of how medical issues were mapped, graphed, and photographed, you can also [narrow the search for resources on this site to just images](https://digital.nls.uk/indiapapers/browse/?format=02), of which there are thousands. This slightly more obscure search mechanic can be used to pinpoint [every mention of Burma the cataloguers have found on specific pages in general surveys](https://digital.nls.uk/indiapapers/browse/?place=1000004%3E1000108), though this is not totally exhaustive of mentions of Burma.
- [Wellcome Trust Archives](https://wellcomecollection.org/works) - The Wellcome trust has over five under digital items [related to Burma](https://wellcomecollection.org/works?query=burma), including copies of some of the vaccination reports available in the NLS above.
- [**British Library Digital Collections related to Burma**](http://explore.bl.uk/primo_library/libweb/action/search.do?fn=search&ct=search&initialSearch=true&mode=Basic&tab=available_online&indx=1&dum=true&srt=rank&vid=BLVU1&frbg=&tb=t&vl%28freeText0%29=burma&scp.scps=scope%3A%28BLWEBSITE%29%2Cscope%3A%28BLO_WA%29%2Cscope%3A%28BLO_Aleph%29%2Cscope%3A%28BLO_SFX%29%2Cscope%3A%28BLO_SAMI%29&vl%282084770704UI0%29=any&vl%282084770704UI0%29=title&vl%282084770704UI0%29=any) **- Use the "Available Online" feature** of the catalogue to identify online materials in the British Library. Other search locations include the Library's [Digitised Manuscripts](https://www.bl.uk/manuscripts/BriefDisplay.aspx).

- [Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek](https://www.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/) - **This library has an extensive collection of materials related to Burma including digital versions of many documents not easily found online elsewhere** but see also their [photos related to burma](https://www.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/searchresults?query=Burma&thumbnail-filter=on&isThumbnailFiltered=true&facetValues%5B%5D=objecttype_fct%3DFotografie&offset=0) as well as several thousand Burma tagged objects in general.

## Digital Newspapers and Journals

Thousands of articles relating to [Rangoon](https://trove.nla.gov.au/search/category/newspapers?keyword=rangoon) and [Burma](https://trove.nla.gov.au/search/category/newspapers?keyword=Burma) are digitised on "[Trove](https://trove.nla.gov.au/)" (mostly Australian papers). Trove similarly has sources from [magazines and newsletters](https://trove.nla.gov.au/search/category/magazines?keyword=burma), [maps, images and artefacts](https://trove.nla.gov.au/search/category/images?keyword=burma&l-artType=Maps&l-availability=y), [reports](https://trove.nla.gov.au/search/category/research?keyword=burma), [books and articles](https://trove.nla.gov.au/search/advanced/category/books?keyword=burma&date.from=1700&date.to=1945), [diaries and letters](https://trove.nla.gov.au/search/category/correspondence?keyword=rangoon&l-availability=y), and [music and videos](https://trove.nla.gov.au/search/advanced/category/music?keyword=Burma&date.from=1700&date.to=1945).

[Straits colony newspapers are digitised](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/newspapers/browse/catalogue) on "Newspaper SG", interesting articles on Rangoon/Burma can be found such as the advertisement of [Burmese funeral trams](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/newspapers/Digitised/Article/straitsecho19070426-1.2.23?ST=1&AT=search&k=rangoon%20tram&QT=rangoon,tram&oref=article) in Rangoon or the [importation of rickshaws](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/newspapers/Digitised/Article/easterndaily19071107-1.2.24?ST=1&AT=search&k=rangoon%20tram&QT=rangoon,tram&oref=article).

Details of newspapers, including their language and the political leanings of their writers, were produced by the government in documents [such as this one](https://www.indianculture.gov.in/archives/statement-newspaper-and-periodicals-published-india-and-burma-during-year-1933-prepared), as well as ones [specific to Burma](https://www.indianculture.gov.in/archives/annual-report-english-and-vernacular-newspapers-and-periodicals-published-burma-year-1937), other editions of which can be found on [Indian Culture.](https://www.indianculture.gov.in/)
