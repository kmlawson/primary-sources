Primary Sources

Lists of open access primary sources for the study of East and Southeast Asian history, aimed primarily at students working with sources in English.

  • Modern Korean History Images and film, colonial reports, missionary records, early periodicals, US government documents, books online, and Korean and Japanese language sources.
  • History of Taiwan Images and film, colonial reports, missionary records, maps, books online, and Chinese and Japanese language sources.
  • History of Modern Shanghai Virtual Shanghai, periodicals, municipal and police reports, guides, directories, maps, films, and memoirs and accounts online.
  • Missionary Perspectives on China Missionary periodicals, medical missionary reports, reference works, memoirs and biographies, photographs, and archival collections.
  • History of Burma Pre-colonial sources in translation, government reports, legal history, gazetteers, censuses, maps, and books online.
  • The Malay World Government reports, maps, newspapers, medical and botanical reports, the National Archives of Singapore, and digitized books.
  • Modern Japanese History Periodicals, yearbooks, guidebooks and maps, with a searchable database of digitized books.
