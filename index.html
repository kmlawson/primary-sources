Primary Sources

Lists of open access primary sources for the study of East Asian history, aimed primarily at students working with sources in English.

  • Modern Korean History Images and film, colonial reports, missionary records, early periodicals, US government documents, books online, and Korean and Japanese language sources.
  • History of Taiwan Images and film, colonial reports, missionary records, maps, books online, and Chinese and Japanese language sources.
  • Modern Japanese History Periodicals, yearbooks, guidebooks and maps, with a searchable database of digitized books.
