# General Online Resources

- [Leiden University Digital Collections](https://digitalcollections.universiteitleiden.nl/) - The digital collections of Leiden University are a valuable starting point for materials, especially on Dutch colonial rule.
    - [Leiden University Southeast Asian & Carribean Images](https://digitalcollections.universiteitleiden.nl/imagecollection-kitlv) - A digital archive with a lot of photographs of Indonesian landscapes, houses, towns, people and customs
- [BookSG](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/printheritage/) - Extensive database with collections of notable colonial and Chinese figures in Malaya and Singapore. Also holds collections of early Malaya, Chinese and Tamil manuscripts.
- [Malaysia Unbound](https://imagined.my/?page_id=622) - Public history project exploring alternative history of Malaysia and Southeast Asia.
- [Cornell](https://guides.library.cornell.edu/c.php?g=471252&p=3258971) - This is mainly a database for other Southeast Asian archive databases. It holds resources for country-specific profiles, newspapers and general Southeast Asian collections.
- *Harvard Digital Collections* | [Malaysia](https://digitalcollections.library.harvard.edu/catalog?utf8=%E2%9C%93&search_field=all_fields&q=malaysia) | [Singapore](https://digitalcollections.library.harvard.edu/catalog?utf8=%E2%9C%93&search_field=all_fields&q=Singapore) | [Indonesia](https://digitalcollections.library.harvard.edu/catalog?utf8=%E2%9C%93&search_field=all_fields&q=Indonesia)
- [Arizona State University](https://libguides.asu.edu/SoutheastAsianStudies) - Mainly a collection of mainland Southeast Asian archival databases (for Laos, Thailand, and Cambodia) but holds some maritime Southeast Asias sources.
- *Northern Illinois University* | [Main Page](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/seadl/) | [Southeast Asia](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/content/general-information) | [Malaysia](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/islandora/search/mods_subject_geographic_lcsh_ms%3A%22Malaysia%22) | [Singapore](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/islandora/search/mods_subject_geographic_lcsh_ms%3A%22Singapore%22) | [Indonesia](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/islandora/search/mods_subject_geographic_lcsh_ms%3A%22Indonesia%22)
- [Columbia University](https://guides.library.columbia.edu/seasia) - This is mainly a database for other Southeast Asian archive databases. It holds resources for country-specific profiles, newspapers and general Southeast Asian collections.
- [University of Washington](https://guides.lib.uw.edu/research/southeastasia) - This is mainly a database for other Southeast Asian archive databases
    - *University of Washington* | [Main Page](https://guides.lib.uw.edu/research/southeastasia) | [Singapore](https://digitalcollections.lib.washington.edu/digital/collection/ic/search/searchterm/Singapore/field/covera/mode/all/conn/and/cosuppress/) | [Indonesia](https://digitalcollections.lib.washington.edu/digital/collection/ic/search/searchterm/Indonesia/field/covera/mode/all/conn/and/cosuppress/) | [Malaysia](https://digitalcollections.lib.washington.edu/digital/collection/ic/search/searchterm/Malaysia/field/covera/mode/all/conn/and/cosuppress/)
- *National University of Singapore* | [Main Page](https://digitalgems.nus.edu.sg/) | [English Language Newspapers](https://digitalgems.nus.edu.sg/collection/1521)
    - [Chinese in Southeast Asia Links](https://digitalgems.nus.edu.sg/collection/87/items)

    - [Chinese Language Newspapers](https://digitalgems.nus.edu.sg/collection/96)

- [National Archives of Singapore](https://www.nas.gov.sg/archivesonline/)
- [ROOTS](https://www.roots.gov.sg/) National Heritage Board of Singapore - Online collection of oral, visual, and material history of Singapore.
- [Journal of the Malaysian Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society (JMBRAS)](https://www.jstor.org/journal/jmalaysianras) - Holds collection of *Journal of the Malaysian Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society (1964-2016)*, *Journal of the Malayan Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society (1923-1963)*, *Journal of the Straits Branch of the Royal Asiatic Society (1878-1988)*. See the valuable overview of issues of these journals with Internet Archive links by Yala Abdullah: [Royal Asiatic Society Journals](https://sites.google.com/view/yala-melayu/royal-asiatic-society-journals?authuser=0)
- [Malaysia Design Archives](https://www.malaysiadesignarchive.org/category/collections/) - A digital archives preserving Malaysian art, photographs, postcards and visual culture
- [CRA gallery - Vintage travel brochures & photographs](https://www.cra-gallery.nl/) - On-line collection related to tourism in the former Netherlands East Indies. The main categories of the collection are Travel Brochures and Photographs.
- [Researching Colonial History Of The Malay World Like A Millennial](https://sites.google.com/um.edu.my/colonialhistoryofthemalayworld/home) - This is a wonderful collection of resources by Simon Soon at the University of Malaya discovered only late in the process of creating our guide here. It offers a wider range and breadth of resources in its links than our guide here, including to many specific libraries, archives, and research communities of interest. The resource also provides detailed and useful guidance to students setting out on their research. This resource list is also especially strong in pointing out resources beyond the English language in its [resources by country](https://sites.google.com/um.edu.my/colonialhistoryofthemalayworld/resources-by-country?authuser=0) section.
- [Visions of Penang Archive](http://visionsofpenang.com.my/collections.php) - A collection of mainly visual resources (photographs, maps, postcards) dedicated to Penang and run by the Georgetown World Heritage Inc.
- [Journal of Malaysian Studies Archives](http://web.usm.my/km/past%20issue.html)
- [SouthEast Asian DEvelopment in the Long Term](https://seadelt.net/Malaysia/) - This is mainly a database for other Southeast Asian archive databases
- [Guide to Southeast Asian Sources in the Eisenhower Library Holdings](http://eisenhowerlibrary.gov/sites/default/files/research/subject-guides/pdf/southeast-asia.pdf)
- *Southeast Asia Digital Archive* | [Main Page](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/seadl/)
    - [Indonesian Television Archive](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/seadl/islandora/object/SEAImages%3AITvVideos)
    - [Indonesian Illuminated Manuscripts](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/seadl/islandora/object/SEAImages%3Alontar)
    - [On the Record: Indonesian Literary Figures](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/seadl/islandora/object/SEAImages%3ALontarVideos)
    - [Online Research Journals for Malaysia](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/seadl/malaysia/journals)
    - [Online Research Journals for Singapore](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/seadl/singapore/journals)
    - [Online Research Journals for Indonesia](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/seadl/indonesia/journals)
    - [The Living Memory Project](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/seadl/islandora/object/SEAImages%3ALMPVideos) - The project was a list of photographs and videos of experiences of East Timorese nationalists who were imprisoned under the Indonesian occupation.
    - [Jawi Translation Project](https://sea.lib.niu.edu/islandora/object/SEAImages%3AJawi#:~:text=The%20Jawi%20Transliteration%20Project%20focuses,Malay%20aspirations%20and%20intellectual%20thought.) - The Jawi Translation Project is an initiative to translate 2000 jawi script newspapers and magazine articles written between 1930-1941 in the Straits Settlements and Malaya. They have been translated from Jawi script to the latin Malay script. Its a translation of four major publications: Saudara, Warta Malaya, Majalah Guru, and Majlis
- [ipohWorld](https://www.ipohworld.org/) - Includes articles, sources, and historical database focused mainly on the history of Perak.
- [Indian Ocean and Maritime Asia Slave Trade Databases](https://datasets.iisg.amsterdam/dataverse/IOMASTD)
- [Singapore War Crimes Trials](https://www.singaporewarcrimestrials.com/) - Archival Material on the Singapore War Crimes Trials
- Key resources especially strong in Dutch sources, but sometimes with English or visual materials:
    - [Delpher](https://www.delpher.nl/) - Massive digital collection of historical Dutch language books, periodicals, and other texts.
    - [KB Nederland](https://www.kb.nl/en) - The national library of the Netherlands (but see Delpher above)
    - [Rijksmuseum](https://www.rijksmuseum.nl/en) - Large online collections from the Museum of the Netherlands in Amsterdam
    - [De Digitale Bibliotheek voor de Nederlandse Letteren](https://www.dbnl.org/)
    - [VOCsite](https://www.vocsite.nl/boeken/)
    - [Geheugen van Nederland](https://geheugenvannederland.nl/)

The US [Office of the Historian](https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/ebooks) offers ebooks of the volumes which are compiled and released as those documents are made available to the public. # Digitized Historical Books Online

**Open Access Books**

Below is a large linked list of some of the many open access books related to the Malay world online (both what you might categorize as primary but also secondary literature). Most of these are available on the [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/), but also on other archives, university libraries, Hathi Trust, or Google Books. Another collection of online books and articles definitely worth consulting is the [Sejarah Melayu Library](http://www.sabrizain.org/malaya/library/index2.html). There is also a very rich collection of ebooks at [BooksSG](https://eresources.nlb.gov.sg/printheritage/).

Most of the works are in English. If our collection continues to grow and include significantly more Malay, Indonesian, Dutch, or other languages, we may add language tags. For your convenience, we have also tagged groups of texts, but note that this tagging process is far from complete. Metadata for these texts was usually pulled automatically from their online home and may contain errors, so please check the sources themselves and entries for them in library databases or check [Worldcat](https://www.worldcat.org/). You may also view this list of works directly via this [Zotero group](https://www.zotero.org/groups/4703692/malay_world/library).
