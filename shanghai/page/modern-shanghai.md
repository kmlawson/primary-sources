# Virtual Shanghai

The best open online resource for the study of modern Shanghai, deserving special mention up front, is the wonderful [Virtual Shanghai](https://www.virtualshanghai.net) project. Below are highlighted only some of the excellent primary source materials there.

- [**Maps**](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/Maps/Collection) - Hundreds of maps of Shanghai over time. See also their [Base Maps](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/Maps/Base) and [eAtlas](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/Maps/eAtlas) collections, as well as GIS data in their [Live Maps](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/Maps/Live) section.
- [**Images**](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/Photos/Images) - Thousands of images from Shanghai, often with very useful metadata.
- [**Films**](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/Films/Collection) - Some hundred films or film clips related to Shanghai.
- [**Elibrary**](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/Texts/E-Library) - This collection of hundreds of documents, contains many extremely important PDF downloads of historical publications and documents related to Shanghai, some of them linked again below.
- **Data** - There is a wealth of data files hosted by the project that are rich references related to Shanghai history. See the hundreds of entries in the [Numerical Data](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/Data/Tables) section, the [Appendices](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/Texts/Appendices), and the extensive database of Shanghai [Buildings](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/Data/Buildings), which often includes addresses, photographs and GPS locations. There is also a list of [Repositories](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/References/Repository) and [Publishers](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/References/Publisher) which connects, in turn, to lists of materials produced by individual publishers related to Shanghai.
- **Secondary Sources** - See the hundreds of entries in the [Bibliography](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/References/Bibliography) section and the publications in the [Papers](https://www.virtualshanghai.net/Texts/Articles) section.

# Periodicals

A few of the most important publications available online and open access in English useful for research on modern Shanghai can be found listed [here](https://www.chinafamilies.net/links-for-further-research/newspapers/historic-newspapers-published-in-china-available-open-access-online/) and include some of those below:

- [North China Herald](https://archive.org/details/northchinaherald) - Over 3,800 issues can be downloaded and browsed. There is a non-open access commercial database that supports full text search.
- [China Weekly Review](https://archive.org/details/thechinaweeklyreview) - Over 1,000 issues in first half of the 20th century.
- [Chinese English Intelligence](https://archive.org/details/hbhyb-archive) - 1947-48, over 200 issues.
- [Far Eastern Review](https://archive.org/details/thefareasternreview) - Several hundred issues from 1904-1941. Published for a time in Shanghai.
- [Israel's Messenger 1904-1934](http://www.jewsofchina.org/israel-s-messenger-1904-1934-en) - partial run of the Official Organ of the Shanghai Zionist Federation, 1904-1934
- [London and China Telegraph](https://archive.org/search?query=title%3A%28London%20and%20China%20Telegraph%29) - Over 600 issues from 1860-77.
- [Shanghai Evening Post and Mercury](https://archive.org/search?query=title%3A%28Shanghai%20Evening%20Post%20and%20Mercury%29) - A few issues of this online.
- [Leo Baeck Institute Library Periodical Collection](https://archive.org/details/lbiperiodicals) (mostly German)

There are over 80 historical Chinese newspapers from Shanghai available online, most of which, of course, are in Chinese:

- [East Asian Newspapers and Periodicals 1850-1950](https://archive.org/details/eastasia-periodicals) - A large Archive.org collection of downloadable newspapers. While slower to browse, the advantage of this platform is that full issues of these can be downloaded easily in PDF.
- [Historic newspapers published in China available Open Access online](https://www.chinafamilies.net/links-for-further-research/newspapers/historic-newspapers-published-in-china-available-open-access-online/) - Broader link list of open access newspapers from China, including many available on Archive.org but also others.
- [Tableau: Index to the Periodicals Published during the Late Qing and Republican Eras](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/yanhe/viz/PeriodicalsinLateQingandRepublicanEraOpenAccess/sheet2) - An interactive map with links to open access collections of Chinese historical newspapers.
