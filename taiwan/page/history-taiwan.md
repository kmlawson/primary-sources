# Images, Audio, and Film

- [East Asia Image Collection at Skillman Library, Lafayette College - Taiwan](http://digital.lafayette.edu/islandora/search/*:*?f[0]=cdm.Relation.IsPartOf:%22East%20Asia%20Image%20Collection%22&f[1]=eastasia.Coverage.Location.Country:%22Taiwan%22) - Over a thousand postcards and photographs, many from Taipei. See also specifically the [Truku-Japanese War Commemorative Postcard Collection](http://digital.lafayette.edu/collections/eastasia/tjwar-postcards/browse?fbclid=IwAR1mZ3WW5NwoZLzNRtDRUgqxAXo0ZJQnibXaxV4hEl_l8Fb2w_ov_tz4aQ0) this includes.
- [Formosa Nineteenth Century Images](https://rdc.reed.edu/c/formosa/home/?fbclid=IwAR2E09-KcgvTBuHLTAA1d93ZZwiT9Cg5WTydhvzplEkUHCLKlQSA6cU9caU) - "This digital library gathers together a large body of primarily European and American images of the island of Taiwan – called "Formosa" by foreign visitors in the nineteenth century – and its various peoples, natural resources, wildlife, and built environment. These textual and visual representations, maps, and linguistic data were originally published in European and North American books and journals during the 19th century, but are not easily accessible to those interested in the history of Taiwan today."
- [National Taiwan University Taiwan Historical Photo Archive](http://photo.lib.ntu.edu.tw/pic/db/oldphoto.jsp) - This has a rich collection of historical photographs related to Taiwan. You will have to enter your search terms in Chinese however. For pictures of Taipei (台北 or 臺北) enter the Chinese version in 簡易查詢 box and press the 送出查詢 button next to it.
- [Taiwan National Repository of Cultural Heritage](http://newnrch.digital.ntu.edu.tw/prototype/index.php) - This search engine works best when you search in Chinese but some English terms produce results too. Includes many old photographs and maps.
- [Taiwan Ethnological Collections in Overseas Museums](http://tcbfm.digital.ntu.edu.tw/#) - The interface is mostly in Chinese but once in you can browse information and photographs of many of the holdings of museums around the world related to the peoples of Taiwan. Great for material history.
- [Vintage Formosa](https://web.archive.org/web/20180216100821/http://www.taipics.com/) - This was the largest collection of historical images of Taiwan. The site has been since discontinued, but archived versions of its collection remain on the Internet Archive.
- *Cushing Library Medical Posters - Taiwan* - Yale University's Cushing Library has a nice collection of over 40 medical posters, mostly from 1950s Taiwan with messages related to hygiene and health. Search by "Department of Education, Taiwan." [This link](http://findit.library.yale.edu/?utf8=%E2%9C%93&id=digcoll%3A2819769&search_field=creator&q=Department+of+Education%2C+Taiwan) should reach the results. Yale's broader digital collection has several hundred maps and images [related to Taiwan](http://findit.library.yale.edu/?id=digcoll%3A2819769&per_page=100&q=Taiwan&search_field=topic&utf8=%E2%9C%93).
- [Sun's wife and Miss Liu jointly manifest the great victory in Taiwan 勝大縣化彰中会姐小劉同會人夫孫](http://digital.soas.ac.uk/LOAA003941/00001) - from SOAS digital collections.
- [Taipei Air Station](https://taipeiairstation.blogspot.com/2019/11/frank-smiths-photos-of-taipei-area.html?fbclid=IwAR0OifbU4wYGD04pXRAv6FwbOXUykexZckR9DjqjILKvR5vvTrUzQ6SbjYc) - Collection of photographs.
- [Wellcome Collection](https://wellcomecollection.org/works) - The digital holdings of the Wellcome collection have many historical photographs from Taiwan if you search [using the term](https://wellcomecollection.org/works?query=formosa&fbclid=IwAR1LP1tc-0dYeoMyvxmP9kXWyuzfH1QrIUC9B2DbVyRgREF7kaI6iu7F1cc) (also by "Formosa").
- [Old Pictures of Taiwan](https://tme.ncl.edu.tw/en/old-photographs?fbclid=IwAR3vLxNE9mE_Irt7MhuAN8_IutFb3cpvL_WYkISdXQWNzMXWolDPxkTnhDw) - Part of the Taiwan Memory Exhibition

# Missionary Reports and Minutes

- *The Christian Movement in the Japanese Empire including Korea and Formosa* - Published by the Conference of Federated Missions, with hefty sections on Korea, with each volume over 600 pages in length. A number of volumes are on Internet Archive: [1913](https://archive.org/details/christianmovemen16conf/page/n9/mode/2up), [1916](https://archive.org/details/christianmovemen00conf/page/n8/mode/2up), [1919](https://archive.org/details/christianmovemen17conf/page/n8/mode/2up), [1920](https://archive.org/details/christianmovemen18conf/page/n12/mode/2up)

Cheng *A Short History of the Presbyterian Church among the Mountain Tribes in Taiwan* (1965). Here is a [list of issues](https://archive.org/search.php?query=title%3A%28report%20of%20the%20Foreign%20Missions%20China%2C%20Formosa%29) on the Internet Archive:

# Wilson Center Digital Archive

The Wilson Center's [Digital Archive](https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org) related to Cold War History is the largest open access collection of historical documents on the Cold War and East Asia is its main focus. There are many documents related to the Republic of China on Taiwan from 1945 onwards. Some of collections particularly relevant include:

- [Asian Peoples' Anti-Communist League](https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org/collection/193/asian-peoples-anti-communist-league)
- [China at the United Nations](https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org/collection/178/china-at-the-united-nations)
- [Taiwan Strait Crises](https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org/collection/177/taiwan-strait-crises)
- [Taiwan’s Cold War in Southeast Asia](https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org/collection/270/taiwan-s-cold-war-in-southeast-asia)
- [The Overseas Chinese and the Cold War](https://digitalarchive.wilsoncenter.org/collection/201/the-overseas-chinese-and-the-cold-war)

# Other Online Archives and Collections

- [Taiwan Documents Project](http://www.taiwandocuments.org/) - "Taiwan Documents Project was established in 1999 to provide researchers and other interested persons a comprehensive source of primary material relating to the dispute over Taiwan. The Project seeks not only to gather together the most complete collection of Taiwan-related documents available anywhere, but also to present objective, introductory analyses of legal and other issues surrounding the Taiwan Strait Situation."
- [V. S. Beausset Collection](http://dtrap.lib.ntu.edu.tw/DTRAP/index.htm) - "Valery S. de Beausset was an influential figure in budget and development planning during the period of U.S. Aid to Taiwan. An engineer and advisor to the J. G. White Corporation, contractor of many U.S.-aided development projects as well as the consulting firm to the U.S. Government at the time, de Beausset became a leading figure in U.S. Aid to Taiwan between 1950 and 1957. He was instrumental in drawing up a list of priorities for granting American aid. As a key coordinator he had close contacts with many Taiwan officials, including Jing-guo Jiang, Jia-gan Yan, Guo-zhen Wu, and Guo-ding Li." This is one of several collection (more below), and though the interface is in Chinese, clicking through to the 狄寶賽文庫 collection offers over a thousand documents, many of which are in English.
- [National Taiwan Museum 國立台灣博物館](https://www.ntm.gov.tw/en/)
- [Taiwan National Treasure 國家寶藏](https://www.nationaltreasure.tw/en) - Digital collection of mostly English language documents related to the history of Taiwan from sources such as the US National Archives, the UN, and the Hoover Institution.
- *The View from Taiwan* - This blog by Michael Turton ran from 2005 to 2018 and is filled with difficult to find historical photographs, quoted passages from historical works, and links to resources related to Taiwanese history and culture.
- [衛城的閱讀空間 Willie Chen's Digest of Historic Places](https://chenwillie.blogspot.com/) - Fantastic focus on the spatial history of Taiwan with photographs of many key locations and links and information on them.
- [漁人碼頭的戰爭 - The Battle of Fisherman's Wharf](https://danshuihistory.blogspot.com/) - Some great links and historical photographs shared here.
- *Taipei Times* - The "Taiwan in Time" series among its features often introduce interesting historical materials, including photographs. One way to find many of these pieces is through a google search: `site:taipeitimes.com/News/feat "Taiwan in Time"` - see also the "Highways & Byways" feature series: `site:taipeitimes.com/News/feat "Highways & Byways"`
- [History of Tea in Taiwan](https://taiwanleaftea.com/info/history-of-tea-in-taiwan)
- [Taiwan Gazette](https://www.taiwangazette.org/) Oral accounts of 2.28 [translated here](https://www.taiwangazette.org/news/2019/3/5/the-228-massacre-in-chiayi-the-airport-and-train-station-were-washed-with-blood?fbclid=IwAR1CSGujoEQumpLrbKgSr07slZfidRQ0DOkGh4nKAmXOpdNVF79pjMYa-Nw).
- [Taiwan Insight](https://taiwaninsight.org/) - Part of the University of Nottingham Taiwan Studies Programme.

# Chinese and Japanese Language Sources

- [Archives of the Institute of Taiwan History, Academia Sinica 中央研究院臺灣史研究所檔案館 - 臺灣歷史檔案資源網](http://archives.ith.sinica.edu.tw/resources_list.php?fbclid=IwAR1z1A4i6fQlKlSlO5OYYfrxB9NwcB7EQd8lC4buiZv7NLU5JRnxCSetjr8) - This is of the richest collections of digitized historical materials related to Taiwan. They include the [臺灣史檔案資源系統](http://tais.ith.sinica.edu.tw/sinicafrsFront/index.jsp), the [臺灣研究古籍資料庫](http://rarebooks.ith.sinica.edu.tw/sinicafrsFront99/index.htm), the [臺灣總督府公文類纂查詢系統](http://sotokufu.sinica.edu.tw/), the [臺灣日記知識庫](http://taco.ith.sinica.edu.tw/tdk/), the [臺灣文獻叢刊資料庫](http://archives.ith.sinica.edu.tw/news_con.php?no=230), and the [臺灣總督府職員錄系統](http://who.ith.sinica.edu.tw/) among other resources and links. Some resources require registration.
- *國史館 Academia Historica* - Together with Academia Sinica and the national archives, one of the three key archival sites for researchers of the Republic of China, both on the mainland and in Taiwan. They now host a range of digital materials open on the web. Some can be seen even without application, if you click on the first image of a document in the reader. Their online archive system is here: [國史館檔案史料文物查詢系統](https://ahonline.drnh.gov.tw/index.php?act=Archive), and look for documents with a blue 線上閱覽 link.
- [國史館臺灣文獻館 Taiwan Historica](https://www.th.gov.tw/new_site/index.php) - Read an [introduction](https://www.th.gov.tw/new_site/01archives/04introduction/) to the materials available or head to their list of databases: [檔案查詢系統](https://www.th.gov.tw/new_site/01archives/01file_archives/). They include colonial period materials in their 臺灣總督府檔案, which you can apply for access to here: [查詢餘額申請表](http://ds3.th.gov.tw/ds3/app000/). They also have their Chiang Kai-shek collection available here: [國史館—蔣中正總統文物](https://across.archives.gov.tw/naahyint/sendurl.jsp?dbid=DB3174)
- [National Central Library 國家圖書館](https://enwww.ncl.edu.tw/) - One of two national libraries in Taiwan.
- [National Taiwan Library](https://www.ntl.edu.tw/) - The other national library, which houses many colonial period historical works in open stacks.
- [Taiwan Memory 台灣記憶](https://tm.ncl.edu.tw/index?lang=chn) - Mentioned above for some of its English language pages, this rich collection of sources, including many photographs, postcards, and books. The books often link to the catalog entry in the National Central Library.
- [National Taiwan University Digital Taiwan-Related Archives Project 臺灣大學深化臺灣研究核心典藏數位化計畫](http://dtrap.lib.ntu.edu.tw/DTRAP/index.htm) - See an introduction to the contents of this collection of digital archives here: [內容簡介](http://dtrap.lib.ntu.edu.tw/DTRAP/intro.htm). There is some background available in English as well [here](http://dtrap.lib.ntu.edu.tw/DTRAP/en/project.htm) but effective use of it requires Chinese. See their online English language [web exhibition](https://www.lib.ntu.edu.tw/doc/CG/resources/project/drapexbi01/e_map_index.html) (and [Chinese](https://www.lib.ntu.edu.tw/doc/CG/resources/project/drapexbi01/index.html).
- [Taiwan Postwar National Historical Archive](http://nhd.drnh.gov.tw/AHDPortal/index.jsp) (Chinese)
- [臺灣轉型正義資料庫 Taiwan Transitional Justice Commission Archive](https://twtjcdb.tjc.gov.tw/) - This newly available (2020) archive contains case files and other information related to repression during Taiwan's period of white terror and authoritarian rule. For more details on the sources and background, see the [使用說明 資料庫內容設定、資料來源、建置過程與編碼工作](https://twtjcdb.tjc.gov.tw/Manual/Detail/1025).
- [二二八事件檔案資料庫](https://228.drnh.gov.tw/) - This archive of materials related to the 2.28 Incident and its aftermath is hosted by Academia Historica. Many of the documents are for request through this system, but a good many of the documents here have scanned images that can be viewed directly in the browser.
- [台灣佛教史料庫 Taiwanese Buddhism Archive](http://buddhistinformatics.dila.edu.tw/taiwanbuddhism/tb/) - This rich collection of archival documents and publications related to buddhism in Taiwan includes online [gazetteers](http://buddhistinformatics.dila.edu.tw/taiwanbuddhism/tb/mq/mq_gaz.html), online archive of the colonial period [南瀛佛教會報 South Seas Buddhist Magazine](http://buddhistinformatics.dila.edu.tw/taiwanbuddhism/tb/ny_new.html), as well as selected postwar materials, bibliographies, and links to other resources.
- [National Taiwan University Digital Projects Home](http://www.digital.ntu.edu.tw/en/achievements.jsp) (Chinese)
- [Taiwan History Digital Library 台灣歷史數位圖書館](http://thdl.ntu.edu.tw/) (Chinese) - This digital library combines sources from a number of distinct collections. To read more about it, see the article [台灣古契約文書全文資料庫的建置](http://thdl.ntu.edu.tw/thdl_pdf/%E5%8F%B0%E7%81%A3%E5%8F%A4%E5%A5%91%E7%B4%84%E6%96%87%E6%9B%B8%E5%85%A8%E6%96%87%E8%B3%87%E6%96%99%E5%BA%AB%E7%9A%84%E5%BB%BA%E7%BD%AE%EF%BC%88%E9%A0%85%E6%BD%94%EF%BC%89.pdf), especially page three for the overview of its holdings. See some of the publications making use of the collection in the 相關論文 below.
- [Research Tools for Taiwan History Digital Library 臺灣歷史數位圖書館研究工具集](http://thdl.ntu.edu.tw/tools/) - Tied closely to the materials housed in the above digital library, this collection of open digital tools include some related to Taiwanese history such as the [臺灣總督府抄錄契約文書歷史地理資訊系統](http://thdlgis.digital.ntu.edu.tw/) (「使用者除可以關鍵字詞，契書種類及契書地點進行檢索外，亦可透過多邊形檢索功能自行框取範圍，並搭配時間軸呈現檢索結果。可供選擇比較的地圖資料包括1904年臺灣堡圖、地形圖、衛星地圖、古今對照及行政區域圖」), the [THDL臺灣總督府抄錄契書地區分析](http://thdltm.digital.ntu.edu.tw/treemap-infovis.php) (「本工具以階層圖形式提供使用者分析並觀察THDL臺灣總督府抄錄契書分布情形。階層圖中外層的區塊為『堡』，內層的區塊為『庄』，區塊越大的堡、庄代表隸屬於此區域範圍的契書數量越多，並同時呈現『庄隸屬於堡』的階層關係。」),
- [台灣原住民族數位典藏資料庫](http://dore.tacp.gov.tw/tacp/pingpu/index.html) - A rich collection of sources, including photographs, oral histories and recordings, as well as material culture objects related to the indigenous peoples of Taiwan.
- [National Taiwan University Library Open Access Books 臺灣大學圖書館 公開取用電子書](http://ebooks.lib.ntu.edu.tw/Home/ListBooks)
- [Taiwan Colonial Court Records](http://tccra.lib.ntu.edu.tw/tccra_develop/) (Chinese/Japanese) - requires online application for access
- [Taiwan Database for Empirical Legal Studies](http://tcsd.lib.ntu.edu.tw/) (Chinese)
- [Digital Library of Local Councils Journals 中華民國地方議會議事錄總庫](http://journal.th.gov.tw/)
- [Digital Repository of Taiwan Provincial Assembly 臺灣省議會史料總庫](https://drtpa.th.gov.tw/) (Chinese) - requires online application for access
- [文化資料庫](http://nrch.culture.tw/Default.aspx) - Both a portal to other resources and host to digital materials, including thousands of old photographs.
- [National Taiwan University Institutional Repository](http://ntur.lib.ntu.edu.tw/) (Chinese)
- [*National Diet Library of Japan Digital Collections*](https://dl.ndl.go.jp/) - Japan's national library has thousands of books related to Taiwan. Search for those which are available online (インターネット公開資料) and you will be able to download PDFs of most of them from earlier periods.
- [JACAR](https://www.jacar.go.jp/) - The Japan Center for Asian Historical Records has many digital records related to Japanese colonial rule in Taiwan. See also their special web exhibit on [The Sino-Japanese War of 1894-1895](https://www.jacar.go.jp/english/jacarbl-fsjwar-e/index.html), after which Taiwan became a colony of Japan.
- [HathiTrust](https://babel.hathitrust.org/) - Especially if you are connecting from the United States, there are hundreds of full view books on the HathiTrust in Japanese (the majority but not all of the English works are already on archive.org where PDF download is simpler) related to Taiwan. If you connect from outside the US many of these full view books have search only restrictions.
- For Japanese materials on colonial Taiwan, see also links and sources covered by Sayaka Chatani in her [Basic Guide to Resources on Japanese Colonialism](http://froginawell.net/guides/japanese-colonialism.html).

There are other great overviews of materials related to Taiwan and more detailed descriptions of some of the resources above. See, for example: Paul D. Barclay. “Japanese Empire in Taiwan.” In the *Oxford Research Encyclopedia of Asian History*. Ed. David Ludden. New York: Oxford University Press, forthcoming.
